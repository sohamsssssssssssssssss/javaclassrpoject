the workshop was quiet when clara arrived on monday morning.
clara put a notebook on the table and opened the nearest window.
a small bird landed outside. the sky was clear, but the road was still wet from last night's rain.
this was the first day of a new project. the team wanted to make the workshop easier for visitors to use.
the computer displayed a list of tools. each item had a name, a date, and a short description.
clara checked the list carefully. one description was missing, and two dates were incorrect.
"what should we do first?" asked a colleague. "let's fix the simple mistakes before we add anything new."
they didn't hurry. they compared each record with a note written by the person who had created it.
by 10:30, the team had corrected 12 records and saved a copy of the original list.
the work became easier after they agreed on a clear method. a careful question often saved an hour of guessing.
jade was the name of their experimental computer program. it learned patterns from examples, not from magic.
the program sometimes made strange suggestions. "we can't trust every answer," clara said.
this reminded everyone to check the evidence. a confident sentence could still describe the wrong thing.
at lunch, they sat near the door and shared bread, apples, and a pot of tea.
once upon a time, the workshop had been a small room with only a wooden table. now it served the whole town.
in the afternoon, clara helped a visitor find information about the tools.
the visitor asked three questions. the first was easy; the second needed a book; the third had no clear answer.
"i don't know yet," clara replied. "we can look at the records together tomorrow."
when evening came, the team closed the windows, checked the lights, and wrote down the next day's tasks.
the project was unfinished, but it was a little more useful than it had been that morning.
